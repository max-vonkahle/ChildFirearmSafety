Research Study

Firearm Safety
Education in Youth

Expert evaluation session for an interactive AI system designed to teach firearm safety to children ages 4–7.

Protocol #8042  ·  ~60 minutes  ·  Remote Session via Zoom

What You'll Be Evaluating
Today's session has three components, evaluated in order. After each, you'll complete a short Qualtrics survey.
01

NAO Robot Module

A social robot delivers a firearm-safety lesson teaching four key steps: Stop, Don't Touch, Run Away, Tell an Adult.

02

AR Training Module

An augmented-reality environment where children practice the four safety steps using gesture-based controls and AI coaching.

03

AR Simulation

Three staged AR testing scenarios in the kitchen, garage, and bedroom where the child must apply the safety steps independently.

Halfway Point

Short Break

Great work — that wraps up the training phase. Feel free to take a moment before we continue to the testing phase.

Phase 2

Testing Phase Evaluation

You'll watch three AR testing scenarios based on the app's staged room prompts: kitchen, garage, and bedroom.

AR In-Situ Simulation — 3 Scenarios
As you watch, pay attention to realism, appropriateness of the system's feedback, tone escalation, and potential for emotional distress.

Scenario 1 — Kitchen

At a friend's house, the child is asked to go to the kitchen to find cookies for a snack.

Scenario 2 — Bedroom

The child is playing hide and seek and decides to hide in the bedroom next to the bed.

Scenario 3 — Garage

At a friend's house, the child is asked to go to the garage to find a toy.

Module 3 of 3 — All 3 Scenarios

AR In-Situ Simulation

Three back-to-back scenarios, ~5 minutes each. The facilitator may pause briefly between scenarios.

Post-AR Simulation Survey

This is the most detailed survey. It covers scenario realism, age-appropriateness, corrective feedback strategy, potential for emotional distress, and implementation feasibility. Take your time.
