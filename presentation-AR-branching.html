Firearm Safety
Education in Youth

Expert evaluation session for an interactive AI system designed to teach firearm safety to children ages 4–7.

~60 minutes  ·  Remote Session via Zoom

IRB Approved  ·  Protocol #8042

What You'll Be Evaluating
Today's session has three modules, evaluated in order. After each, you'll complete a short Qualtrics survey.
01

NAO Robot Guided Training Module

A social robot delivers a firearm-safety lesson teaching four key steps: Stop, Don't Touch, Run Away, Tell an Adult.

02

Interactive AR Training Module

The child verbally recites the safety rules, then physically acts them out in AR with an AI voice coach.

03

Interactive AR Testing Module

Three staged AR testing scenarios in the kitchen, garage, and bedroom where the child must apply the safety steps independently.
